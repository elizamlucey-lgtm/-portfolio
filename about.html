How I Approach Documentation

Good documentation is more than technically accurate content. It should help the reader accomplish something.

I approach documentation by first understanding the audience, the user's goal, the underlying technology, and the workflow the documentation needs to support.

From there, I focus on information architecture, clarity, consistency, usability, and maintainability.
